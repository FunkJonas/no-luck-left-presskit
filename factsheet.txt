No Luck Left is a first-person slot machine roguelite. You stand in a shady casino in front of a slot machine and punch the big red button. Every round is a wave with more health than the one before, and your winning lines deal the damage. Take the wave down before your spins run out, or the house sends its crew after you.
Every win rattles coins out onto the floor. Pick them up, buy symbols and helpers, and grow your machine from 3x3 to a full 5x5 with 48 paylines.

Features:
- A slot machine roguelite where every spin counts
- Grow your machine from 3x3 to 5x5 with up to 48 paylines
- Coins land on the floor – collect them by hand, with a vacuum, robots or a drone
- 12 symbols, 12 helpers and a boss every five waves
- Permanent upgrades in the workshop between runs
- Daily challenge with a Steam leaderboard
- 7 languages, Steam Deck and controller support
- No real money, nothing to buy in the game
